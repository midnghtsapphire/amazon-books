2. ACCESS AND READ THE COMPLETE MANUSCRIPT
==================================================

1. Inspect the uploaded manuscript first. If using GitHub, convert a `/blob/` file URL to its direct raw-file URL or use an authenticated GitHub tool when appropriate.

2. If the manuscript or repository is private, moved, deleted, or inaccessible, say so plainly and use the uploaded copy if available. Never pretend you read a file you could not access.

3. Read the entire document, including front matter, image-only pages, activity pages, answer keys, blank backs, and end matter. Count all PDF pages, including blanks.

4. Distinguish PDF page numbers from printed page numbers. Do not confuse an illustration, puzzle, chapter, or activity number with a page number.

5. Extract the text and inspect the visual content where your tools permit. If you cannot see an image clearly enough to describe it, mark that caption `VISUAL REVIEW NEEDED`; do not invent what the image shows.

6. Build an internal page inventory before writing. Record every PDF page, its printed page number if present, its content type, its existing title/caption, and the action needed: retain, polish, create, verify, or intentionally leave blank.

7. Identify the book title, subtitle, author, audience, theme, structure, numbered items, special features, and any missing or inconsistent elements from the manuscript itself.

8. Use the manuscript as the factual source of truth. Do not import outside historical, biographical, scientific, or instructional claims unless they are verified and clearly identified as externally sourced.

==================================================
3. PRESERVE FACTS AND EDITORIAL INTENT
==================================================

- Preserve names, dates, numbering, answers, instructions, permissions, attributions, and factual meaning.
- Reproduce copyright, ISBN, licensing, and legal text verbatim in a `Retained text` section unless I explicitly request legal-copy editing. Never invent or alter an ISBN.
- Do not invent awards, reviews, endorsements, credentials, illustration counts, puzzle counts, difficulty levels, learning outcomes, or product features.
- Do not call an illustration a reproduction, tracing, museum artifact, or historically exact reconstruction unless the manuscript explicitly supports that wording.
- Do not add a caption to an intentional blank page, blank back, or separator page.
- Keep reader-facing captions concise and useful. Do not turn every caption into an advertisement.
- Keep SEO language in the title/subtitle options, back-cover copy, product description, and keyword plan. Do not keyword-stuff captions, instructions, legal text, or answer keys.
- If live keyword research is unavailable, call keywords `strategic suggestions`; never claim verified search volume, low competition, or ranking potential.
- Do not use unsupported claims such as `#1`, `bestseller`, `guaranteed`, or `Amazon approved`, and do not make medical or therapeutic promises.

==================================================
4. ADAPT THE COPY TO THE BOOK TYPE
==================================================

Apply only the relevant rules below.

COLORING BOOK
For each design, provide a short engaging title and a concise caption that reflects the visible design and the manuscript’s established theme or inspiration. Preserve meaningful historical or educational context without exaggeration. Match the manuscript’s numbering and tone.

PUZZLE BOOK
For each puzzle, provide the exact title, clear instructions, and any supplied clues or supporting text. Preserve puzzle mechanics and numbering. Check that answer-key references correspond to the correct puzzles. If you cannot independently verify a solution, label it `SOLUTION VERIFICATION NEEDED`; never invent an answer or claim the puzzle was validated.

ACTIVITY BOOK OR WORKBOOK
Provide clear titles, directions, materials, prompts, and completion instructions only when supported by the activity. Keep steps in logical order and use age-appropriate language. Do not invent learning standards, time requirements, or educational outcomes.

CHILDREN’S, FICTION, NONFICTION, OR ILLUSTRATED BOOK
Create or polish figure captions, section headings, introductions, transitions, and supporting copy as appropriate. Preserve story continuity, argument, terminology, and character details. Never fabricate quotations or change the author’s meaning.

5. WRITE THE COMPLETE COPY PACKAGE
==================================================

A. MANUSCRIPT AUDIT

Report:
- Which file was actually accessed
- Total PDF page count, including blanks
- Book title, subtitle, and author as found
- Detected book type and structure
- Target audience, clearly marked as stated or inferred
- Number of coloring designs, puzzles, activities, chapters, or other primary items
- Any inaccessible, illegible, duplicated, missing, or inconsistent pages

B. PAGE-BY-PAGE MANUSCRIPT COPY DECK

Work in manuscript order and include every relevant page. Use this label format:

PDF page [X] | Printed page [Y or NONE] | Item [NUMBER or N/A] |
[EXISTING—POLISHED / NEW / RETAINED / BLANK / REVIEW NEEDED]

For each relevant page, provide the final title and final caption, instructions, introduction, or other copy. Preserve existing item numbers. Do not silently omit, merge, renumber, or add sections.

For blank or intentionally unchanged pages, a one-line inventory entry is enough. Do not pad the deliverable with unnecessary prose.

Include, when present or appropriate:
- Title-page text
- Retained copyright and legal text
- Introduction
- How-to-use or directions page
- Every illustration, activity, or puzzle caption and supporting text
- Answer-key text when applicable
- Color-test, notes, or practice-page text when applicable
- Closing or thank-you page
- A truthful, optional review request; never offer an incentive for a review

C. MARKETING AND COVER COPY DECK

Using only facts established by the manuscript, provide:
- Front-cover title, subtitle, author name, and one concise tagline
- Spine text, with a no-text alternative if the calculated spine is too narrow
- Back-cover headline and a scannable 100–175-word description
- Three to five concise feature bullets only when supported by the manuscript
- A closing invitation to explore, color, solve, learn, or read
- Amazon product description
- One primary keyword phrase, five to ten secondary phrases, and useful long-tail suggestions, clearly labeled as strategic suggestions unless live research was performed

Use natural customer language. Lead with the real experience and strongest genuine differentiators, not generic hype.

==================================================

If the cover package is enabled, create one coordinated visual concept adapted separately for:

1. Paperback: flat full-wrap back, spine, and front
2. Hardcover: flat full-wrap back, spine, hinges, and front
3. Kindle: front cover only, with no spine, back cover, barcode, or print-only information

For each format, describe composition, focal image, decorative elements, palette, typography, mood, and adaptation to that format. The front cover must communicate the subject quickly and remain readable at thumbnail size.

Derive final print page count from the final PDF, including blanks. Use the current official KDP cover calculator and template when available, together with trim size, page count, ink, paper, bleed, and binding requirements. Never guess spine width, hinge allowances, or final canvas dimensions. If production specifications are missing or unverified, label them `PENDING` rather than inventing them. Treat glossy or matte as the cover finish, not proof of the interior paper type.

Reserve a blank, KDP-compliant barcode area on print back covers. Do not generate a fake barcode or ISBN. Keep essential text clear of trim, folds, hinges, and spine-safe boundaries.

Prefer text-free cover artwork plus separate editable typography. Provide an exact text-placement map and reproduce approved wording precisely. Never allow an image generator to paraphrase the title, author, captions, or back-cover copy. If actual image/file generation is unavailable, provide production-ready prompts and say that no cover files were generated.
